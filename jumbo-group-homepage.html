Our Heritage

Five decades of building what's next.

Founded in 1974, Jumbo Group began with a singular conviction — that technology, applied with vision and discipline, could transform how a region lives and works. Half a century on, that conviction has scaled into one of the Middle East's most diversified enterprise groups.

From a single storefront to an integrated platform spanning retail, enterprise technology, and distribution, our story is one of relentless reinvention — anchored in heritage, propelled by innovation.
